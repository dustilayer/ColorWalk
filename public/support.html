反馈问题

请说明设备型号、浏览器和系统版本、操作步骤及错误文字。截图请遮住个人信息；不要发送密码、令牌或私人照片。

保存与删除

网页档案保存在当前浏览器。清除网站数据或换设备可能无法找回作品，请先导出重要记录。未保存的拍摄内容可能因刷新或关闭页面丢失。出现保存或删除问题时请保留现有数据并联系支持。

隐私与统计

当前设置开关不控制结束页的 Supabase 统计调用，不能以关闭开关作为停止全部上报的保证。详见隐私政策。本页面提供网页版本支持，不承诺 iOS 应用已经上架。

Support

Operator: Lingjia Zhu. Email the address above with your device, browser/OS version, steps and error text. Redact personal information in screenshots; do not send passwords, tokens or private photos.

Web archives are stored in the current browser. Clearing site data or switching devices may lose access to your work. Export important records first; unsaved captures may be lost on refresh or closing the page. Preserve existing data and contact support if saving or deletion fails.

The current settings toggle does not control the end-page Supabase reporting call. Turning it off does not guarantee all reporting stops. See the privacy policy. This page supports the Web app and does not claim an iOS release is available.
