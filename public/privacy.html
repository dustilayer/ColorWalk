ColorWalk 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月24日

运营者：Lingjia Zhu
隐私与支持联系：dustilayer@126.com

本说明适用于 colorwalk.org 的 ColorWalk 网页版本。它不描述尚未核实的 HueRove iOS 应用或 Apple 购买流程。

相机与本机保存

拍摄功能请求相机权限，不请求麦克风。保存档案时，照片写入本浏览器的 IndexedDB，文字及颜色档案写入 localStorage；偏好、成就和本机累计统计也保存在浏览器中。网页没有自动上传照片的流程。未保存的采集内容可能在刷新或关闭页面后丢失。

可在档案中删除记录，也可使用浏览器的清除网站数据功能。删除或存储失败可能留下不完整记录；不能仅凭界面变化保证所有照片已删除。清除网站数据可能使作品无法恢复，请先导出重要内容。下载或分享由你主动发起，副本由你选择的系统或服务管理；删除本机档案不会撤回这些副本。

漫步统计与设置开关

当前网页代码在漫步结束页会尝试发送一条使用统计；部署中配置了 Supabase 时，接收方为 Supabase 的 walk_reports 数据表。报告字段为每次报告随机生成的 UUID、模式、严格程度、时长（秒）、颜色数量、浏览器语言分类以及应用版本。报告内容不包含照片或具体颜色值，但网络服务还可能接收 IP 地址和请求头等信息，不能据此宣称完全匿名或不收集数据。

当前设置里的统计开关并不控制上述结束页上报。关闭该开关不能保证停止此项发送。实际部署是否启用 Supabase、哪些报告已接收及后台保留情况尚未核实；本说明不将未核实的情况表述为没有发送或没有历史数据。

浏览器还使用 sessionStorage 标记本次会话内已尝试上报的记录。清除本机数据不会删除可能已发送到服务端的记录。

托管、字体与缓存

网站由 Vercel 托管。加载页面和资源会向托管服务发送网络请求。应用主页通过 Google Fonts 加载字体，可能连接 fonts.googleapis.com 和 fonts.gstatic.com。这些服务可接收 IP 地址、请求地址、浏览器请求头等网络信息。

Service Worker 在浏览器中缓存应用资源、字体及音频以支持后续加载。本隐私页与支持页使用系统字体，不包含追踪脚本、外部字体或上传表单。网站和第三方服务的日志保留期限、处理地区及项目实际设置尚未核实，因此这里不承诺固定期限、特定地区或零日志。

支持邮件与隐私请求

如果你主动联系支持，邮件服务商及收件人会处理你提供的邮箱地址、正文和附件以答复问题。请只发送必要信息，不要发送密码、交易令牌或私人照片。

如需询问处理情况、请求访问、更正或删除，请向上述邮箱发送主题为“ColorWalk 隐私请求”的邮件，说明请求涉及的数据和大致时间。本机档案可直接在应用或浏览器中处理。随机报告标识未与邮箱绑定，可能无法仅凭邮箱定位历史统计；已分享给其他服务的副本需联系相应服务。本说明不承诺无法核实的删除结果或固定支持邮件保留期限。

ColorWalk Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026. Operator: Lingjia Zhu. Privacy and support: dustilayer@126.com. This notice covers the ColorWalk Web app at colorwalk.org, not unverified HueRove iOS or Apple purchase flows.

Camera and local records

The camera feature requests video, not microphone access. Saving an archive writes photos to browser IndexedDB and text/color records to localStorage. Preferences, achievements and local counters also use browser storage. The Web app has no automatic photo-upload flow. Unsaved captures may be lost on refresh or closing the page. Delete archives in the app or clear site data in browser settings. Storage/deletion failures may leave incomplete records, so a changed interface does not guarantee complete photo deletion. Export important work first. Downloads and sharing are initiated by you; deleting local records does not recall copies held by your chosen service.

Walk reports and the settings toggle

The current code attempts to send a report when a walk ends. When Supabase is configured in the deployment, it sends to the Supabase walk_reports table: a random per-report UUID, mode, strictness, duration in seconds, color count, browser-language category and app version. This payload does not contain photos or specific color values. Network services may additionally receive IP addresses and request headers; this is not a claim of complete anonymity or no data collection.

The statistics toggle in Settings does not control this end-page reporting path. Turning it off does not guarantee reporting stops. Whether Supabase is enabled in the actual deployment, which reports have been received and backend retention remain unverified. This notice does not treat those unknowns as proof of no reporting or no historical data.

sessionStorage marks attempted reports within a browser session. Clearing local data does not delete reports that may already have reached a server.

Hosting, fonts and caches

Vercel hosts the site. Loading pages and assets sends requests to the host. The app homepage loads Google Fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. These services may receive IP addresses, requested URLs and browser headers. A service worker caches application resources, fonts and audio in the browser. This privacy page and the support page use system fonts and contain no tracking scripts, external fonts or upload forms. Actual project settings, service-log retention periods and processing regions have not been verified; no fixed period, specific region or zero-logging claim is made.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, your email service and the recipient process the address, message and attachments you supply to respond. Send only necessary information, not passwords, transaction tokens or private photos. For processing questions or access, correction or deletion requests, use the subject “ColorWalk privacy request” and describe the data and approximate time involved. Manage local records through the app or browser. Random report IDs are not linked to email addresses, so an email alone may not identify historical reports. Contact the relevant service about shared copies. This notice makes no promise of unverified deletion results or a fixed support-email retention period.
