HueRove iOS 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月24日

运营者：Lingjia Zhu
隐私与支持联系：dustilayer@126.com

本政策适用于 HueRove iOS 应用。ColorWalk 网页版及本网页的访问处理另见 网站隐私政策；网页统计不代表此 iOS 版本的处理行为。

相机与本机数据

相机用于你主动拍摄和取色，不请求麦克风或位置权限。照片、草稿、档案信息、偏好、成就和使用计数保存在应用本机存储中。当前版本没有自动上传照片、漫步统计、设备标识或诊断数据到开发者服务器的功能。界面语言根据系统语言或你保存的选择确定。

你可以在应用中删除档案和草稿。保存或删除失败时请重试；尚未成功写入的内容可能丢失。卸载或清除应用数据可能删除作品，请另存重要内容。应用没有自有照片云同步或找回服务。

分享与系统备份

主动分享时，应用生成临时文件并交给你选择的系统或服务，之后尝试清理缓存；清理失败可能留下副本。应用内删除不会撤回已分享的副本。

iOS 备份和设备迁移可能包含应用数据，取决于系统及用户设置。“本机保存”不表示数据永不进入系统云备份，也不保证备份能够完整恢复。备份及分享目标的处理适用相应服务的规则。

Apple 与购买

当前版本不提供应用内购买。应用没有将 StoreKit 交易上传到开发者服务器的实现。运营者目前未下载或使用 Apple 提供的销售、订阅或诊断报表。

Apple 的 App Store、系统服务和备份可能自行处理数据，适用 Apple 的隐私说明；以上表述不表示 Apple 不处理数据。未来开放购买或改变报表使用方式时，将相应更新本政策。恢复购买如在未来开放，用于核验权益，不会恢复已删除的照片。

支持邮件与隐私请求

主动发送邮件时，邮件服务商和收件人会处理你提供的邮箱地址、正文及附件。运营者仅将这些邮件用于回复和处理请求，不转交作其他用途。事项办结后邮件仍会保留；本政策不承诺固定保存天数或自动到期删除。

请只提供必要信息，不要发送密码、交易令牌或私人照片。如需询问数据处理或提出访问、更正、删除请求，请联系 dustilayer@126.com 并说明涉及的数据。本机档案可通过应用删除；已分享给其他服务的副本需由相应服务处理。发送删除请求不表示删除已自动完成。

HueRove iOS Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026. Operator: Lingjia Zhu. Privacy and support: dustilayer@126.com.

This policy covers the HueRove iOS app. The ColorWalk Web app and visits to this hosted page are covered separately by the website privacy policy. Web reporting does not describe the processing performed by this iOS version.

Camera and local data

The camera is used for photos and color capture you initiate, without requesting microphone or location access. Photos, drafts, archive information, preferences, achievements and counters are stored locally in the app. The current version has no functionality that automatically uploads photos, walk statistics, device identifiers or diagnostic data to developer servers. Interface language follows system preferences or your saved choice.

You can delete archives and drafts in the app. Retry after save or deletion failures; content not successfully written may be lost. Uninstalling or clearing app data may delete your work. Keep separate copies of important content. The app has no photo cloud-sync or recovery service of its own.

Sharing and system backups

When you initiate sharing, the app creates a temporary file and passes it to your chosen system or service, then attempts cache cleanup. Failed cleanup may leave a copy. Deleting in-app data does not recall shared copies.

iOS backups and device migration may include app data according to system and user settings. Local storage does not mean data can never enter system cloud backups or guarantee complete recovery. Backup and sharing destinations follow their own processing rules.

Apple and purchases

The current version does not offer in-app purchases. The app has no implementation that uploads StoreKit transactions to developer servers. The operator has not downloaded or used sales, subscription or diagnostic reports supplied by Apple.

Apple's App Store, system services and backups may process data independently under Apple's privacy information. Nothing above means Apple processes no data. This policy will be updated when purchases are enabled or report usage changes. If purchase restoration becomes available, it will recheck access, not restore deleted photos.

Support email and privacy requests

If you email us, email providers and the recipient process the address, message and attachments you supply. The operator uses these messages only to respond and handle requests and does not pass them on for other purposes. Messages are retained after a matter is resolved; this policy promises neither a fixed number of retention days nor automatic expiry-based deletion.

Send only necessary information, not passwords, transaction tokens or private photos. For processing questions or access, correction or deletion requests, contact dustilayer@126.com and describe the data involved. Manage local archives through the app; copies shared with other services must be handled by those services. Sending a deletion request does not mean deletion has automatically completed.
